\section{A Small Diagram Algorithm}

Algorithm 1 computes the writhe when the signed crossings of an oriented diagram are already known.

\begin{algorithm}[H]
    \caption{Compute the writhe of a knot diagram}
    \label{alg:encoding}
    \begin{algorithmic}[1]
        \Require A list $S=(\varepsilon_1, \ldots , \varepsilon_m)$ with $\varepsilon_i \in \{-1, +1\}$
        \Ensure The writhe $w(D)$
        \State $w\gets 0$
        \For{each $\varepsilon$ in $S$}
            \State $w \gets w+\varepsilon$
        \EndFor
        \State \Return $w$
    \end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

The algorithm performs one addition per crossing, so
its running time is
\begin{equation}
    T(m) = \Theta(m)
\end{equation}
where $m=c(D)$.
