\section{ Reidemeister Moves}

Two diagrams represent equivalent knots exactly when
one can be changed into the other by planar deformation
together with the three Reidemeister moves \cite{kauffman1987}:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\Roman*)]
    \item Twist move: adds or removes one loop and one crossing
    \item Poke move: adds or removes two neighboring crossing;
    \item Slide move: moves one strand across a crossing.
\end{enumerate}
These moves preserve the underlying knot type even
though the visible number and arrangement of crossings
may change.
